# **App Name**: Akashdeep Eye Centre Inspired

## Core Features:

- Home Page with Slider: A landing page featuring a prominent image slider showcasing the clinic's services and ambiance.
- About Us Page: Detailed information about the clinic's mission, values, and the expertise of the medical team.
- Services Overview: Comprehensive overview of eye care treatments offered, including Cataract Surgery, Glaucoma Treatment, LASIK, and more.
- Service Detail Pages: Dedicated pages for each treatment, providing in-depth information, benefits, and potential risks of the service, determined via a generative AI tool.
- Online Appointment Booking: Interactive form allowing patients to easily request and schedule appointments online.
- Testimonials Section: Showcase of patient testimonials to build trust and credibility.
- Contact Information: Easily accessible contact details, including address, phone number, email, and a map.
- Responsive Design: Fully responsive design adapting seamlessly to various screen sizes and devices.
- Before and After Gallery: A gallery showcasing before-and-after photos of patients who have undergone successful treatments.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Teal (#008080) to evoke professionalism, trustworthiness, and eye health.
- Secondary color: Light gray (#D3D3D3) for a clean and modern background.
- Accent color: A vibrant blue (#007BFF) to highlight important information and CTAs.
- Body font: 'Roboto' for clear and modern readability.
- Headline font: 'Montserrat' for impactful headlines.
- Use clear, medical-themed icons from FontAwesome or similar libraries.
- Clean, spacious layout with clear sections and consistent design elements, resembling the Akashdeep Eye Centre website.
- Subtle animations and transitions to enhance user experience and engagement.
